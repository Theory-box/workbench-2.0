Black exterior shadow follow-up (2026-11-04):
Reproduced in Azola Mezzo November 2026 v2 testing.blend, frame 9. The shader
cache treated texture-unit bindings like per-program uniform values. Switching
between regular/instanced material shaders could overwrite a cached shader's
shadow-map binding, causing a material texture to be sampled as shadow depth.
Rebind uShadowMap when returning to an initialized program. Keep matrix/scalar
uniform caching. Also invalidate tracked shader state after planar overlays.
The targeted fix matches the full-uniform-rebinding reference pixel for pixel
in the actual scene at 480x270. A small mixed regular/instanced regression
reproduces 6220 incorrect pixels in the old path and zero error with the fix.
Evidence: parent outputs/workbench-black-shadow-fix.json and before/after PNG.
The saved user scene and installed add-on remain unchanged; no push or release.
