First scope: selected coplanar faces, active scene camera, sharp single-bounce
reflection, explicit Bake/Rebake. Store camera and plane transforms with image.

Reuse existing evaluated scene, mesh GPU buffers, materials, and textures.
Queue capture in an active renderer draw callback, where the owning GPU context
and prepared geometry exist. Do not instantiate another scene or RenderEngine.

a shadow target if the current shadow coverage does not cover the reflected view,
and the persistent baked image. Avoid permanently retaining both CPU/GPU copies
of intermediate capture data. Use bulk pixel transfer, not Python pixel lists.

- Store baked image with blend; indicate camera-specific validity.
- Test wall/room behind mirror, tilted mirror, orthographic camera, scaled
